What can you actually do with the information?

What you discover, and what you do about it
What you discoverWhat you do
A feature's costs doubledInspect recent prompt or traffic changes
One customer consumes disproportionate tokensInvestigate usage patterns or introduce plan limits
A small model performs adequately for simple tasksRoute suitable requests to that model
The same document is summarised repeatedlyCache the result
Retries account for significant spendingFix retry loops and enforce limits
AI costs increase faster than revenueAdjust pricing or feature allowances

The goal is not just showing costs, but helping teams lower their cost per useful AI task.

How is this different from OpenAI's own dashboard?

Providers already offer usage reporting, cost exports, spend alerts and project spend limits, so a total-spend dashboard is not enough.

AI Spend Guard combines several providers and connects costs to the application feature, customer and result.

It should answer

  • Which customer is most expensive to serve with AI?
  • What does it cost us to resolve one support ticket?
  • Which model gives the best balance of cost and acceptable output?
  • How much money did our prompt optimisation actually save?
Limitation Provider billing data alone won't identify features or customers; application-level instrumentation is needed, and estimates must account for model pricing, retries, cached tokens and other billable operations.

Could you use this with your existing AI tools?

LLM API usage

Instrument API calls and aggregate cost by team, workflow and customer.

Developer-assistant subscriptions (Copilot, Claude subscriptions)

These bill via pooled licences or plans, may not expose per-request cost, and need separate integrations. The SDK alone won't capture them.

Recommendation

The promise

Know the cost of every AI feature and customer, and get alerted before spending becomes a problem.

Build v1 around that promise

  • One LLM provider
  • A lightweight SDK
  • A cost dashboard
  • Budget alerts
  • A customer/feature breakdown

Add model recommendations and automated cost controls later.

Key validation question

Will developers pay for that visibility when providers already offer basic usage reporting? Interview potential customers before committing the full 2-4 weeks.
